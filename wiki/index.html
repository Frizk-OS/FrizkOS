Welcome to FrizkOS Wiki

The official knowledge base for the FrizkOS project.

FrizkOS is a custom-built, bare-metal oriented operating system designed to bring back absolute control over hardware. We strip away modern bloatware, proprietary telemtry, and heavy abstractions, relying on our custom OneCore API and direct C/C++ native bridges to communicate with the kernel.
